About Royal Interior

Transforming Houses into Bespoke Luxury Sanctuaries in Ghaziabad

At Royal Interior, we believe every space holds potential for extraordinary elegance. Based in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, our team of passionate interior architects and craftsmen blend contemporary trends with timeless classic aesthetics.

From Indirapuram to Raj Nagar Extension and across the NCR region, we offer comprehensive interior design services including space planning, luxury furniture manufacturing, lighting design, and turnkey execution.
